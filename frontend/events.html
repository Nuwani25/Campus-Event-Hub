Tips for Organizers

📅 Plan Ahead

Create your event at least 2 weeks in advance to give attendees time to register.

✨ Descriptive Details

Provide clear information about what attendees can expect and any requirements.

👥 Know Your Audience

Consider which departments or student groups would be most interested in your event.

📣 Promotion

Share your event on social media and department notice boards.

📊 Feedback

After your event, encourage attendees to submit feedback to improve future events.
